# Black Box Recorder

**Category:** pwn
**Difficulty:** easy

A damaged onboard flight system was recovered after a suspicious emergency landing.  
Most of the aircraft controls are dead, but one maintenance interface is still alive and accepting pilot log entries.

Ground control believes the software was never meant to survive direct contact with a human typing random junk into it.  
Maybe the black box recorded more than telemetry.

Your mission: interact with the flight computer and see whether you can gain access to restricted maintenance functionality.
